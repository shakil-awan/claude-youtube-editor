Cursor's paid plan costs twenty dollars a month. GitHub Copilot Pro costs ten. Copilot Pro includes unlimited code completions, plus fifteen dollars of AI credits every month. Cursor lists extended agent limits, but no dollar amount. Both have a free tier. Copilot Free gives you two thousand completions and fifty chat requests. Cursor Hobby needs no credit card. So if you mostly want autocomplete and some agent work, Copilot Pro is ten dollars cheaper. If agents are your whole workflow, test Cursor's free plan first. Ten dollars a month. That is the gap.
