## Full narration (spoken order)

Zapier charges thirty dollars a month after your first hundred tasks. Hit that cap, and you're
paying for every workflow you run. Here's the setup: Zapier's free plan gives you one hundred
tasks, then the cheapest paid tier is thirty dollars for just seven hundred fifty tasks. What if a
tool had no task limit, and cost nothing? That tool is n8n. Self-host the free community edition
and there's no task cap in the license — it runs as many workflows as your computer can handle,
forever, for free. Zapier still charges thirty dollars for tasks n8n gives away for free. Follow
for the free tool that replaces your next subscription.
