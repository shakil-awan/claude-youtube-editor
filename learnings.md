numbers have proven. When they disagree, this file wins — it's calibrated on OUR channel.

Rules for updating (for the skill, or a human):
- Only add a lesson when the data supports it across **≥ 3 videos** — one video proves nothing.
- Every lesson cites the videos behind it (id + views + avg % viewed).
- Superseded lessons get struck through, not deleted — the history is the calibration record.
- Keep it under ~40 lessons; merge or prune when it bloats.
