Firefox just killed the nineteen dollar AI browser subscription. It's called Smart Window, powered by Mistral AI, built right into your browser. Ask it to dig through your open tabs, summarize a page, or answer a hard question, and it just does it. No subscription, no credit card. Mistral promises zero data retention, so your chats aren't stored on their servers. Compare that to Merlin, a popular AI browser copilot that charges nineteen to twenty nine dollars a month for the same thing. It's rolling out now in the US, Canada, and France. Free AI, built into your browser. The nineteen dollar subscription is dead.
