# study-competitors — adopt what the top is doing, on purpose

Watching the market is a process, not a vibe. This skill runs a structured sweep, deconstructs
winners, and turns observations into **testable patterns** that `/write-short` actually uses.
Hard line: we adopt *patterns* (hook shapes, pacing, structures), never *content* (their script,
their visuals, their thumbnails). Copying is both a YPP risk and a ceiling on quality.

## The sweep (monthly, ~30 min)

1. **Find the winners** (write every finding into `videos/research/competitors-YYYY-MM.md`):
   - Breakout Shorts in the niche, last 30 days — vidIQ MCP outliers when connected
     (`contentType: short`, keyword "ai tools"), else YouTube search sweeps sorted by views.
   - The 5–8 channels behind them: subs, cadence, format mix, what they DON'T do.
   - Note which are faceless + TTS like us — those are the direct comparables.
2. **Deconstruct the top 10 Shorts** — watch each and record in the doc:
   - **Hook**: exact first line + first-frame visual; which of our hook rules it confirms/breaks.
   - **Pacing**: seconds per tool, total beats, where it breathes.
   - **Visuals**: screen recording / stock / generated? caption style? watermark? progress bar?
   - **Packaging**: title pattern, hashtags, pinned comment, description links (affiliate?).
   - **The gap**: what would make OUR version of this better (usually: real generated UI demos
     + specific numbers — our structural advantages).
3. **Extract 3–5 patterns** worth testing, each phrased as a testable bet:
   e.g. "top 3 comparables open with a price number on frame 1 — test '$0' as the first visual".
4. **Write to `learnings.md`** under a `## Market patterns (from /study-competitors)` section:
   date-stamped, max 5 active patterns, each marked `untested` → `/write-short` tries them →
   `/shorts-report` promotes them to real lessons (with our own data) or retires them. Market
   patterns NEVER override lessons proven on our channel — our data outranks their example.
5. **Tell the owner** the 3 headlines: who is winning, what changed since last month, what we
   are testing next.

## Budget note

vidIQ credits are limited (check `vidiq_balance` first) — one outlier call per sweep, spend the
rest of the sweep on free YouTube search/watching. The deconstruction is the value, not the list.
