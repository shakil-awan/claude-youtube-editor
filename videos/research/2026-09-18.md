### Why #7 won (Suno vs Udio, versus, short-050)
- **Provability (5):** every number is pulled from the vendor's own site/help center today —
  Suno's free tier ("50 credits per day", "No commercial rights") and paid tiers ($8/mo Pro,
  $24/mo Premier) from `suno.com/pricing`; Udio's free-tier cap ("10 daily credits" + "100"
  monthly, "three 130-second songs per day") and paid tiers ($10/mo Standard, $30/mo Pro) from
  `help.udio.com/en/articles/10739134-credits-and-credit-limits`. No aggregator-only claims made
  it into the script — where secondary sources disagreed (Udio's per-plan commercial-rights split,
  clouded by an ongoing UMG/Warner licensing transition with disabled downloads) the claim was
  dropped rather than risk a wrong fact.
- **Money (4):** direct stock-music-subscription-replacement angle for creators/small businesses
  ($15–50/mo tools like Epidemic Sound/Artlist are the implicit comparison).
- **Learnings-fit (4):** hook leads with two hard numbers and two named products ("50" vs "3"),
  matching the "named product + hard number" pattern learnings.md credits for the news-jack
  outperformance, applied here to an evergreen versus.
- **Saturation (4):** neither tool nor this specific pairing appears on our channel or in recent
  competitor chatter searched today.

### Why #8 won (Chatbase vs Fin, replacement, short-051)
- **Money (5):** the clearest dollar gap of any candidate — Intercom Fin's own pricing page states
  "$0.99 per outcome" with a "50 outcomes per month minimum" (= $49.50/mo floor) vs Chatbase's
  free plan at literally $0/month ("50 message credits/month", "1" agent, no card).
- **Provability (5):** both numbers are direct quotes fetched today from `fin.ai/pricing` and
  `chatbase.co/pricing` — first-party vendor pages, not aggregators.
- **Learnings-fit (5):** the strategy's own replacement-format example ("Stop paying for X — this
  free tool does it") plus a hard dollar figure in the hook.
- **Saturation (4):** AI customer-support agents are untouched on our channel; the closest prior
  topics (Fireflies/Otter, Circleback) are meeting-notes tools, a different job entirely.
