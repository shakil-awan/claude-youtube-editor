Opus Clip and Descript both give you sixty free minutes a month to turn long videos into clips. Same number, very different catch. Opus Clip renders up to full HD, but every free export carries a watermark, and you can only upload local files, no YouTube or Twitch links on the free plan. Descript caps your export at seven twenty p, also watermarked, but throws in five gigabytes of cloud storage. Pick Opus Clip if resolution matters more. Pick Descript if you want to keep your files without paying. Same sixty minutes, very different trade-off.
