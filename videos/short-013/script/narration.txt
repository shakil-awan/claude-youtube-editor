Cloudflare just open sourced an AI that sorts your work for free. It's called Clef. You hand it a customer message and ask, is this urgent, and which team should take it. It answers in about two hundred milliseconds. Cloudflare says that is more than twice as fast as the rival it tested against. The weights are Apache two point zero on Hugging Face, so you can download them and run it yourself. Or use it on Workers AI. Cloudflare just open sourced the free AI that triages your inbox.
