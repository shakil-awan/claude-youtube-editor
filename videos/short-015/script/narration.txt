OpenAI just launched a near-flagship model at one fifth the price. It is called GPT six point one Sol, and it dropped September twenty-ninth at DevDay. Input costs two dollars per million tokens. Output costs ten. Astra, the flagship, costs ten dollars in and fifty out. That is exactly one fifth. Cached input falls to ten cents. OpenAI says Sol matches Astra on its software engineering test at about a fifth of the cost. The catch? Those benchmarks are OpenAI's own, and Sol is in the API, Codex and ChatGPT Work, not regular chat yet. Near flagship brains, one fifth the bill.
