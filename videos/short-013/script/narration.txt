LLM Gateway just launched something free that most AI startups charge for. It's called Smart Routing. You send one request, and it automatically picks the best model out of up to thirty, routing easy questions to cheap models and hard ones to frontier models. The routing itself costs nothing, zero platform fee. You only pay for whichever model it picks, plus a tiny classifier fee if you turn that on. It launched September twenty fifth, it's in open beta, and it even works on pay as you go accounts. If you're burning money sending every prompt to your most expensive model, this fixes that for free.
